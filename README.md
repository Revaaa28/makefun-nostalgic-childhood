## The entry

> I fell asleep in the back seat with my cheek against the cool window, my father's hand keeping time on the steering wheel to a song I still hum without knowing its name.

169 characters, one sentence. Chosen from 12 candidates (see `candidates.md` in the work notes) by manual ranking against the judge's brief, in its stated order: nostalgia felt first, then concrete grounding, honest detail, a tight sentence, and a warm ache that lingers. The line wins its pairwise checks against the field's likely clichés (grandmother's kitchen, summer evenings) on the brief's first criterion — the ache is a loss inside the warmth: a song carried for decades whose name is gone.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the fixed, authored sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no input files — everything the entry runs is in this repository.

